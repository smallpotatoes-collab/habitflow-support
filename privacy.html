Privacy policy

Effective October 31, 2026 · HabitFlow

HabitFlow is an iPhone habit tracker. This policy explains how the app handles information and how to contact the developer.

Data stays on your device

HabitFlow stores habit names, rules, schedules, preferences, check-ins, and evaluated progress locally on your iPhone. It has no account system, app backend, app-managed cloud sync, advertising, or third-party analytics SDK. The app does not automatically upload habit or Health data to the developer or a third party. The developer cannot remotely access or erase your local habit database.

Apple Health and iPhone motion

If you connect an automatic goal, HabitFlow requests access to the supported Health types or motion information used by your habits. It reads measurements to evaluate goals and retain local progress history. It never writes Health measurements. It does not use Health or fitness information for advertising, marketing profiles, or data brokerage. Access is optional; manual check-ins remain available. You can review or revoke access in Health and iPhone Settings.

Screen Time

With your authorization, HabitFlow uses Apple's Family Controls and Device Activity frameworks to monitor thresholds for selected apps, categories, and websites. Selection tokens and saved threshold outcomes are stored locally, including in an on-device shared container used by the app and its extensions. Apple's report extension displays private usage information without exporting raw usage data to the main app. HabitFlow does not send app selections or usage information to the developer and does not block other apps.

Notifications and local preferences

If you allow notifications, HabitFlow schedules local habit reminders. Preferences such as appearance are saved on the device. The app does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking. Current versions do not request location access. Older stored location-rule information, if present after an upgrade, remains local and is no longer used for live tracking.

Sharing you choose

If you use the system share sheet, information you choose to share is handed to the destination you select. The recipient's practices apply after you share it. HabitFlow does not otherwise send that information to the developer.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you change or delete it, delete the app, or restore a backup. You can delete a habit and its associated saved results from the habit's action menu. Removing HabitFlow does not remove the original Health records. HabitFlow marks its complete local database, including imported Health measurements and automatic progress history, as excluded from device and iCloud backups. It does not use CloudKit or upload Health data to a server.

Device backups

A separate backup copy contains only your habit settings and manual check-ins. Apple may include that copy in device or iCloud backups according to your settings. After a restore, automatic history can be rebuilt from available source records; reconnect permissions and reselect Screen Time activities as needed. Backups made by earlier app versions may still contain their app data. Manage or delete existing backup copies through Apple. Apple's backup services are governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Support messages and this website

If you email the developer, your email address, message, and any attachments are used to respond and investigate the issue. Send only the information you choose to share. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain support records; you can ask for its deletion. The developer's email provider processes those messages. This static website contains no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising, cookies, contact form, or externally loaded fonts.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its handling of website information. Visiting this website does not give GitHub access to your Health or habit data in the app.

Children and general wellness

HabitFlow is a general-audience habit tool, not a service designed specifically for children. It does not knowingly collect children's information through the app. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Changes

The policy may be updated when the app's practices change. A revised version will show a new effective date on this page.

Questions or privacy requests

Contact HabitFlow support at goodmeatsoftware@gmail.com. You can also visit the support page.
