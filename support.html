Common questions

How do I create a habit?

Tap + on Today. Choose a template or Custom Habit, name it, set its rule and task days, then save. For supported automatic goals, enable Apple Health Sync and connect the data source when prompted. You can use manual tracking without connecting Health or motion.

Why has my Health goal not updated?

Check that the relevant measurement appears in the Health app and that HabitFlow has permission to read it. Reopen HabitFlow and use Settings → Refresh available history when appropriate. Watch records may arrive after syncing. Apple Health does not reveal whether read access was denied, so no samples can also mean permission is off or no data has been recorded. Manual completion remains available.

Can HabitFlow record water, meditation, or sleep itself?

HabitFlow evaluates supported records already available in Health; it does not write Health measurements. Use another app or device to record quantities or sessions first, or check the habit in manually.

Why are older days unavailable?

Health goals can import available retained measurements. iPhone motion supplies up to seven days of recent history. Screen Time cannot reconstruct days from before monitoring began. Permission changes, deleted source data, or interrupted monitoring can leave gaps. History imports continue in the background.

How do Screen Time goals work?

In the habit's Goal Value editor, tap Choose apps, categories, and websites, authorize Screen Time, and select activity. “At least” goals can complete after a tracked threshold is reached. “Below” goals finish after a fully monitored window closes. iOS controls event delivery and private usage reports. Reopen HabitFlow to refresh saved outcomes. These goals measure usage; they do not block apps.

Can I complete or undo a habit manually?

Yes. Use the completion control for the habit on Today. You can also open the habit to review its rule and history. A manual check-in is useful when a source measurement is missing.

How do I change reminders or appearance?

Edit a habit to enable its reminder and choose a time. Allow notifications in iPhone Settings. In HabitFlow Settings, choose System, Light, or Dark appearance.

How do I pause or delete a habit?

Open the habit, then use its action menu to pause, archive, or delete it. Deleting removes that habit and its associated saved results. It does not delete the original measurements in Apple Health. Deleting the app removes its local app data; device backups are managed separately by Apple.

Is there an account, cloud sync, or subscription?

The current app has no account, app-managed cloud sync, subscription, advertising, or analytics SDK. Habit data is stored and evaluated on the iPhone. Device or iCloud backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.
